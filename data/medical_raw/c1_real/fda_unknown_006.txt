=== PURPOSE ===
To minimize adverse acute and chronic vaccination stress reactions.

=== INDICATIONS AND USAGE ===
Indications To minimize adverse acute and chronic vaccination stress reactions.

=== DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ===
Dosage Unless otherwise prescribed, adults take 5 drops orally in liquid, 1 time per day. Children take 3 drops orally in liquid, 1 time per day.

=== WARNINGS ===
Warning If symptoms persist, contact a licensed practitioner. If you are pregnant or nursing a baby, seek the advice of a health care professional before use. Keep out of the reach of children.
