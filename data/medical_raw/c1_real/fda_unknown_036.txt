=== PURPOSE ===
Antifungal

=== INDICATIONS AND USAGE ===
Uses proven clinically to cure most: athlete's foot (tinea pedis) ringworm (tinea corporis) Effectively soothes and relieves: itching burning cracking helps prevent most athlete's foot from coming back, with daily use There are no major changes. This is a new submission.

=== DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ===
Directions Clean the affected area and dry thoroughly apply a thin coat over affected area twice daily (morning and night) supervise children in the use of this product for athlete's foot, pay special attention to the spaces between toes. Wear well-fitting, ventilated shoes, change shoes and socks daily use daily for 4 weeks. If condition lasts longer, ask a doctor to prevent athlete's foot, apply to the feet once or twice daily this product is not effective on the scalp or nails
