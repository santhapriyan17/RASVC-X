=== PURPOSE ===
PURPOSE - OTC - PURPOSE SECTION Antiseptic

=== INDICATIONS AND USAGE ===
USES - INDICATIONS AND USAGE SECTION Health care personal hand rub to help reduce bacteria that potentially can cause disease

=== DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ===
DIRECTIONS - DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION SECTION Place enough product on hands to cover all surfaces. Rub hands together until dry. Supervise children under 6 years of age when using this product to avoid swallowing.

=== WARNINGS ===
WARNINGS - WARNINGS SECTIONS For external use only. Flammable. Kepp away from heat or flame.
