=== PURPOSE ===
Purpose Purpose: Hoarse cough

=== INDICATIONS AND USAGE ===
Indications Indications: To be used for acute self-limiting conditions according to standard homeopathic indications

=== DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ===
Directions Take at onset of symptoms. Repeat every 2 hours or as needed until relieved. If condition persists or worsens discontinue use and consult a practitioner. Adults: dissolve 5-10 pellets in 1 oz. of filtered water or take dry by mouth. Children and infants: 1-5 pellets.

=== WARNINGS ===
Warning If pregnant or nursing, consult a practitioner before using.
