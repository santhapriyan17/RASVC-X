=== PURPOSE ===
Purpose External analgesic

=== INDICATIONS AND USAGE ===
Uses for temporary relief of pan and itching due to: minor burns sunburn minor cuts scrapes insect bites minor skin irritations rashes due to poison ivy, poison oak, poison sumac

=== DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ===
Directions adults and children 2 year of age and older - apply to affected area not more than 3 to 4 times daily children under 2 years of age - do not use, consult a doctor for best results dry skin thoroughly before applying
