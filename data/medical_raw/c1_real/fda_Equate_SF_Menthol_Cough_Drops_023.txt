=== PURPOSE ===
Purpose Cough suppressant / Oral anesthetic

=== INDICATIONS AND USAGE ===
Uses temporarily relieves: cough as may occur with a cold occasional minor irritation and sore throat

=== DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ===
Directions adults and children 5 years and over - dissolve 1 lozenge slowly in mouth. Repeat every 2 hours as needed or as directed by a doctor. children under 5 years - ask a doctor

=== WARNINGS ===
Warnings Sore throat warning: if sore throat is severe, persists for more than 2 days, is accompanied or followed by fever, headache, rash, swelling, nausea, or vomiting, consult a doctor promptly. If sore mouth symptoms do not improve in 7 days, see your dentist or doctor promptly. These symptoms may be serious.
