=== PURPOSE ===
Use Help prevent and temporarily protects chafed, chapped, or cracked skin.

=== INDICATIONS AND USAGE ===
Baby XtraCare® Oatmeal Daily Moisturizing Lotion Lightly Scented Dimethicon Skin Protectant with Natural Oat Extract Helps Soothe & Soften Baby's Skin 12 FL OZ (354 mL)

=== DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ===
Directions Apply liberally as often as needed.

=== WARNINGS ===
Warnings For extenal use only. When using this product avoid contact with eyes. In case of eye contact, flush with water. Stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens. Symptoms last more than 7days or clear up and occur again within a few days.
