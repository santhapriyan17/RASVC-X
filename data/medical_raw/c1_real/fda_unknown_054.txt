=== DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ===
Directions Apply to underams only.

Warnings For external ues only. Do not use on broken skin. Stop use if rash or irritation occurs Ask a doctor before use if you have kidney disease KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
