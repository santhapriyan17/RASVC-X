=== PURPOSE ===
PURPOSE Purpose: Antiseptic

=== INDICATIONS AND USAGE ===
Uses Uses: Hand mist sanitizer to help reduce bacteria that potentially can cause disease.

=== DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ===
Directions Directions: • Spray enough product on hands to cover all surfaces. Rub hands together until dry. • Spray it to hands, feet, clothing, and shoes wherever need them. • Supervise children under 6 years of age when using this product to avoid swallow or spray it on their eyes.

=== WARNINGS ===
WARNINGS Warnings: For external use only. Flammable. Keep away from heat or flame -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Do not use • in children less than 2 months of age • on open skin wounds -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- When using this product keep out of eyes, ears, and mouth. In case of contact with eyes, rinse eyes thoroughly with water. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation or rash occurs. These may be signs of a serious condition.
