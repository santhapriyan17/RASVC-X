=== PURPOSE ===
Purpose Antibacterial

=== INDICATIONS AND USAGE ===
Use For handwashing to decrease bacteria on the skin

=== DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ===
DIRECTIONS: Pump into hands, lather vigorously for at least 15 seconds, rinse and dry thoroughly.

=== WARNINGS ===
WARNINGS: FOR EXTERNAL USE ONLY. Avoid contact with eyes, if eye contact occurs, flush with water. Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation and redness develops. KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN. If swallowed get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
